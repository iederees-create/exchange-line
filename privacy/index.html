Privacy notice

Your enquiry information

Last updated: 5 August 2026

What we collect

We collect the contact and business phone requirements you choose to provide, plus basic referral information such as the page URL and campaign tags. We do not use detailed browser fingerprinting.

Why we use it

Your details are used to respond to this request, assess fit, prepare a requirements brief, support the formal-quote process and, if you proceed, coordinate onboarding, installation and support.

An inbound request is not marketing consent. It is not automatically added to marketing. Any future marketing permission must be a separate, optional choice.

Who sees it

Authorised Exchange Line personnel and the relevant Premitel contacts may receive the information needed to respond or prepare a formal quote. Operational providers may process information only to provide secure hosting, authentication or transactional delivery.

What we do not collect here

The public site and ordinary application database do not collect bank account numbers, ID numbers, bank statements, proof of bank account, proof of address, debit-order details or copies of sensitive documents.

Retention and choices

Enquiry records are retained only as long as needed for the sales, installation, support, legal and operational purposes that apply. You may ask for access, correction or deletion where applicable by replying through the same email or WhatsApp channel used for your enquiry.

Security

Access to internal records and customer cases is authenticated and role-based. Customers can only access cases linked to their own account.

Contact

Use the email or WhatsApp conversation associated with your enquiry to raise a privacy question. No telephone number is published on this website.
