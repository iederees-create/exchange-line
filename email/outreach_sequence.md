# Exchange Line - Outreach Sequence

Three short, problem-led emails spaced about four business days apart. Every
message must use an honest sender identity, a visible unsubscribe route and a
specific observation about the prospect's business.

Public emails must not include Premitel rates, bundle codes, technical add-on
prices, unsupported savings claims or instructions asking the prospect to
design PBX features.

## Email 1 - The problem check (Day 0)

**Subject:** quick question about {{company_name}}'s business calls

Hi {{contact_name}},

I came across {{company_name}} while looking at businesses in your area:
{{fit_reason}}.

If calls are being missed, difficult to route, or hard to support across the
team, I have a short phone-system check that focuses on the business outcome,
not technical terminology:

{{quote_link}}#calculator

Iederees Francis
Premitel freelance sales

## Sequencing rules

1. Use only approved, lawfully sourced business contacts.
2. Stop immediately on reply, unsubscribe, complaint or bounce.
3. Check `do_not_contact = false` before every send.
4. Skip weekends and keep volume conservative.
5. Never treat an inbound quote request as consent to unrelated marketing.
6. Obtain Premitel's required approval for public or outreach material before use.
