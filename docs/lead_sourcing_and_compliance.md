# Lead Sourcing and Compliance

## Prospect fit

Prioritise South African businesses with a genuine operational call problem:
missed calls, multiple offices, remote staff, weak routing, limited usage
visibility, or reliance on mobile phones and legacy landlines.

Use only accurate public business information. Do not buy consumer lists or
guess personal contact details.

## Public process

Exchange Line does not publish Premitel rates, bundle codes, bundle prices,
technical add-on prices or calculated estimates. Competitors can misuse those
details, and prospects should not be expected to design a PBX.

The public phone-system check collects:

- staff members needing a line;
- office locations;
- current setup;
- desired business outcomes; and
- contact details for an inbound response.

Iederees confirms the requirements directly. Louise at Premitel then prepares
the suitable configuration and formal quote privately.

## Inbound requests and consent

A form submission is an inbound request for a response. It is recorded with
`source = landing_calculator` and `consent_basis = inbound_request`. It is
not marketing consent and must not automatically enrol the person in outbound
sequences. Anonymous browser users may insert a request but may not read,
update or delete lead records.

The WhatsApp questionnaire opens a prefilled message for review. Nothing is
sent until the visitor presses Send inside WhatsApp.

## Outreach and POPIA

Use honest sender identity, a real business reason and a working opt-out.
Suppress contacts immediately after an opt-out, complaint or bounce. Keep
`do_not_contact` authoritative.

Premitel-related public and outreach material must receive the contractually
required approval before use. Never promise a rate, saving, bundle or technical
configuration outside the formal quote prepared by Premitel.
